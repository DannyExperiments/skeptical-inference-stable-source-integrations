# Q622 source and prior-work audit

Checked November 27, 2026. **High confidence** in the target semantics and the distinctions below. **Moderate confidence** that no matching classification appears in the works inspected. The search does not establish absolute priority.

## Exact target

Liberatore's *Belief Integration and Source Reliability Assessment* (JAIR 63, 2018) defines ordered flattening and prefix maximality in Definitions 1–4, p. 93. Its Lemma 1 equates membership in a maxcon with individual consistency relative to that maxcon for every input formula. Section 5, p. 109, defines the tripartition induced by a maxcon and says strong stability requires that every maxcon of a proposed tripartition induce it again. In Section 7.1, p. 133, the merging operator disjoins all maxcons obtained from stable partitions, so skeptical consequence means entailment by each selected maxcon. Section 8.6, p. 137, explicitly asks about the complexity of inference and of strongly stable tripartition existence. We use the strongly stable three-class specialization and the catalog's classical universal empty-case convention. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.11238) · [Institutional PDF](https://iris.uniroma1.it/retrieve/e383531d-3eea-15e8-e053-a505fe0a3de9/Liberatore_Belief-integration_2018.pdf) · [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.02160#page=16)

The reliable class contains sources all of whose statements are individually consistent with the maxcon. The journal prose and proof of Theorem 11, p. 110, confirm that direction, and the author's preprint Definition 8 has the corresponding nonentailment sign. The construction uses internally inconsistent sources, which the model allows. For example, the counterexample to strong existence uses sources whose reports have incompatible truth-set patterns; the author-preprint discussion of Theorem 15 explicitly constructs such sources. The hardness instances here use disjoint, nonempty sources with each formula individually satisfiable. All three tiers can be required nonempty in the promise construction.

## Closest existing results

- Liberatore's Theorem 2 identifies the maxcons selected by singleton-source bipartitions with the plain maxcons. Corollary 1 in Section 4 gives the analogous union for weakly stable bipartitions. Theorem 11 gives one plain-maxcon-to-induced-tripartition direction. These do not establish that a strongly stable tripartition selects every plain maxcon. The 2018 paper also gives instances with no strongly stable tripartition, so the nonvacuous existence promise is a substantive restriction.
- Theorem 10 identifies strongly stable **bipartition** maxcons as maxcons of a larger prioritized formula base whose initial tier consists of conjunctions of the sources. Lemma 11 realizes some prescribed graphs of weakly stable tripartitions. It is a structural realization from explicitly specified patterns and does not give a polynomial reduction from a succinct quantified Boolean formula to skeptical inference under strong stability. [Journal article, Sections 4–5](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.11238)
- Fixed-priority cautious consequence has a long history. Nebel's prioritized base revision (KR 1991, Theorem 15) is $\Pi_2^{\mathrm P}$-complete and uses a supplied priority order. Benferhat et al. distinguish inclusion-based preferred subtheories from cardinality variants. Eiter and Gottlob established $\Pi_2^{\mathrm P}$-complete propositional circumscription deduction. These theories do not themselves quantify over self-supporting source priority orders. [Nebel, Theorem 15](https://gki.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/nebel-kr91.pdf) · [Benferhat et al.](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1993/0090.pdf) · [Eiter and Gottlob](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(93)90073-3)
- Liberatore (Synthese 2022) derives ordinary maxcon merging from robustness over all reliability profiles in a weighted-distance framework; that universal robustness does not filter profiles by the strong fixed-point requirement from 2018. [Belief merging in absence of reliability information](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-022-03750-7)

## Scope of this release

Q622 uses the same quantified-Boolean-formula gadget as the companion Q621 strong-existence result. The unrestricted hardness follows immediately by asking a fresh literal. The nonvacuous case adds a marker-guard construction whose true marker branch always gives an explicitly known strongly stable tripartition, while the false branch faithfully retains the older existence condition. This is a dependent reduction and strengthening of the inference result.

Searches used the journal, the author preprint and later bibliography, fixed-priority inference, weak and strong stability, prioritized circumscription, existence, skeptical consequence and subsequent citations by title. No exact skeptical classification for this fixed-point family or the existence-promise variant surfaced. Alternative notions of mild stability and restricted propositional languages are outside the statement.
