# Claim scope

- The inputs are finite nonempty sources of general propositional formulas and a propositional query. A maxcon is inclusion-maximal at every priority prefix.
- The quantification is over every maxcon of every strongly stable tripartition, as specified by Liberatore's fixed-point criterion. Ordinary universal entailment is true on an empty collection.
- Hardness holds for the separately constructed promise instances even if a guaranteed stable tripartition is made explicit. They use a literal query and disjoint sources of at most three individually satisfiable formulas, with all three tiers nonempty.
- The proof allows internally inconsistent sources. It gives no classification for jointly consistent sources, restricted formula languages or other stability notions.
- The same quantified-Boolean gadget underlies Q621 and Q622. The marker extension yields the nonvacuous inference result. The replay is finite and no proof-assistant formalization is included.
